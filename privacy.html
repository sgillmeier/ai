Basic page counts

GoatCounter is used for aggregate reach statistics. Opening a main page sends its fixed page name. No visitor or session identifier is assigned. The measurement sets no cookies and sends no complete URLs with query parameters, screen measurements or content you enter. Repeat requests may be counted more than once: the figures are not a count of distinct people.

The purpose is to learn whether this portfolio is being used. Basic non-profiling counts rely on the legitimate interest in that feedback (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can disable them below. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are also honoured.

Additional statistics require your choice

Only after you select “Allow additional statistics” does the website count chapters visible for ten continuous seconds, opened MindFlow cards, image enlargements and video interactions. Automatic playback and manual actions are distinguished. Only predefined event names are counted: no mouse recording, conversation content or personal journey. Sources are reduced to broad categories such as LinkedIn, CV channel, search or unknown.

This additional measurement relies on your voluntary consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where applicable, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Without permission, all website content and controls remain available. You can change your choice at any time for future requests.

Provider and technical information

The endpoint is sgillmeier.goatcounter.com. GoatCounter.com is operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland. The provider states that its servers are hosted by Hetzner in Finland and Germany. Requests necessarily disclose an IP address and HTTP browser information to the provider. According to its privacy description, raw IP addresses and full user-agent headers are not permanently stored in the statistics. Depending on account settings, technical groups such as browser families or countries may be stored as separate aggregates. This integration does not request persistent recognition.

Statistics remain in the account until deleted or until its configured retention limit. The provider states that backups may retain data for up to 30 days after account deletion. Collected reports are not published as a visitor database.

When browser storage is unavailable, the statistics choice applies to the current document only.
