Apple Maps · Data quality & integration

One place card.
Two worlds of data.

A country and a restaurant reveal the difference: making Wikipedia knowledge usable, and selecting suitable data providers for everyday places.

My task was not simply to display existing data. Classification, selection, filtering and a suitable source for each market were central.

Structuring knowledge

Wikipedia & Wikimedia Commons

The Switzerland view shows country and continent, population and area, images and a Wikipedia description.

↗

  1. Connect information

    Link Wikipedia content to places, geographical relationships and metadata.

  2. Select images

    Semantically classify Wikimedia Commons images, then select and arrange them for relevance and visual quality.

  3. Filter content

    Develop and apply filters for sexually explicit and violent imagery together with the teams.

Comparing coverage

POI providers · Tripadvisor

The restaurant view visibly attributes photos to Tripadvisor and displays a Tripadvisor rating.

↗

  1. Analyse providers by market

    Compare coverage from Yelp, Tripadvisor, business directories and other providers by country and region.

  2. Fill the gaps

    Include restaurants, hotels and businesses beyond Wikipedia coverage.

  3. Make the source visible

    Identify the selected provider in the place card. In this example, the Tripadvisor contributions are labelled directly.
