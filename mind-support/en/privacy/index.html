Privacy

Using the app

Exercises and instructions work offline. There are no accounts, ads or usage analytics. The app sends me no exercise or health information.

On your iPhone

Language and display settings, and any words or next steps you choose to save, stay in the app. Local counts and timestamps help time the rating request. Deleting the app removes this data from your device. Earlier copies may remain in device backups, depending on your settings.

Rating on Apple’s platform

If iOS shows a rating request, you decide whether to rate the app with Apple. I receive no link between your rating and your exercises.

When you visit these pages

GitHub Pages serves these pages and logs IP addresses for security. GitHub manages its technical logs. These pages load no portfolio analytics, external fonts or advertising. GitHub may also process connection data in the United States. Its privacy statement explains its international transfer mechanisms and retention criteria.

Your optional vote

Only when you submit does your browser send a fixed event to the counting service GoatCounter. The service necessarily receives your IP address and browser information. We send no name, exercise content, browsing history or personal identifier. A flag in this tab’s session storage prevents a repeat vote in the same tab. It expires with the session. The total counts responses and cannot identify individual people. GoatCounter states that it stores neither raw IP addresses nor full browser identifiers in its statistics database. Its privacy information describes its infrastructure and retention.

Who is responsible and your rights

Dr. Stephan Gillmeier is responsible. For privacy questions or requests, contact mindsupportapp@icloud.com. Our legitimate interest in running a secure site is the legal basis for delivery and security (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Your consent is the basis for sending your optional vote (Article 6(1)(a)). You may leave before submitting. Afterwards, an individual vote cannot be linked back to you. Totals are kept to inform decisions about future content. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing and complain to a data protection authority.
