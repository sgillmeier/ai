Mind Support

A gift for your everyday life

When so much is happening in the world at once, I find it helps to have a little room to look closely and try something for myself. Perhaps you do too.

I spent several months making Mind Support so that familiar exercises and ideas would be easier to see and try. This app is my gift to you. It’s free, and you can go at your own pace.

Dr. Stephan Gillmeier

What you can explore

Follow a breathing rhythm, gently tense and release a muscle, or watch how a thought can turn into a loop. The Learn tab gives you room to explore these ideas.

You choose

Choose an exercise that feels right for you today. You can keep your eyes open, pause or stop at any point. The content is for learning and practice. It does not provide diagnosis, medical advice or therapy, or replace care from a professional.
